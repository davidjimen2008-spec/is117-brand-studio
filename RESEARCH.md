## 2. Two positions compared

| | **Position A — "Basics first" (Uniqlo-like)** | **Position B — "Identity first" (Stüssy/Essentials-like)** |
|---|---|---|
| Core claim | A tee is a dependable everyday tool; let the wearer add the style. | A tee is a way to signal who you are; the brand supplies the attitude. |
| Look | Neutral, product-centered, clear sizes and prices. | Bold type, lookbook photos, logo as a feature. |
| Strength for my audience | Fits "nice everyday clothes that are not too extra"; builds trust. | Fits "advanced style of normal clothes"; memorable. |
| Risk | Can feel too ordinary (the tension in my SPEC). | Can feel too over-the-top, or imply a status I cannot back up. |
| **My decision** | **Mostly A for structure and honesty** | **Borrow B only for headline, type and logo attitude** |

This matches what I already chose in SPEC.md: one centered white tee, a bold left-aligned headline from the Postmodernist preview, and no hard sell.

## 3. Two short persona hypotheses (from SPEC.md, labeled as assumptions)

**Persona 1 — Primary: the everyday streetwear wearer.** Wants nice everyday clothes that are not too extra and to feel "an advanced style of normal clothes" (my stated goal). Tension: basics feel too ordinary, bold styles feel too much. Needs to decide whether the brand is distinctive without being loud.

**Persona 2 — Secondary: the plain-tee comparer.** Is comparing plain tees and wants to confirm sizes and what the demo bag does before bothering. Needs clear size choices and an honest "demo, not an order" message.

*Evidence status:* both are hypotheses. Persona 2 was AI-proposed and I adopted it. Neither is based on interviews or visitor data.

### Chosen audience: **Persona 1 (everyday streetwear wearer)**
**Why:** The three sites show an open gap between ordinary basics (Uniqlo) and loud or exclusive streetwear (Stüssy, Essentials). Persona 1 describes someone who sits in that gap, and it matches my brand character (The Everyman) and the slogan EVERYWO/MAN'S ATM. Persona 2 stays as a check on the same page, not a second design.

## 4. Look, brand character, honest persuasion

- **Look:** blue and neutrals, white tee at the center, bold headline, serif accent, simple A-with-arrow wordmark (A = average, arrow = better than normal).
- **Brand character:** The Everyman. Voice traits: outgoing, striking, meaningful.
- **Honest persuasion (already on the site):**
  - *Reciprocity* — "Three ways to wear the white tee with clothes you already own." Free styling ideas, no signup, no product claims.
  - *Unity* — "Everyday style has room for every point of view." Invites identification without claiming an existing community.
- **Deliberately not used:** scarcity (restock alerts, "final sale"), invented reviews or counts, unsupported fabric, fit or sustainability claims.
