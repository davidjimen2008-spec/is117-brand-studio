## Ethical persuasion

Choose two applications that help the visitor make a voluntary, informed decision. Record their real implementation and mechanism rather than a principle name alone. Required size selection or validation alone does not establish consistency. Do not invent social proof, scarcity, authority, or product facts.

| Principle                                    | Actual copy or interaction                                       | Visitor benefit / factual support                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Learner-selected implementation: Reciprocity | “Three ways to wear the white tee with clothes you already own.” | Free, optional styling ideas offer practical inspiration without signup or a purchase. The suggestions do not claim product properties. |
| Learner-selected implementation: Unity       | “Everyday style has room for every point of view.”               | Invites identification with the learner-described shared everyday style; it does not claim an existing customer group or community.     |
